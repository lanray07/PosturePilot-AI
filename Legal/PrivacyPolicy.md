# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

PosturePilot AI is a wellness and productivity app for posture habits, desk ergonomics awareness, focus sessions, movement reminders, and local insights.

## Data Collection

The current app version stores posture sessions, focus sessions, stretch routine completion, desk setup scan notes, achievements, preferences, and subscription state locally on the user's device with SwiftData.

The current app version does not sell personal data, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not collect analytics for third-party advertising.

## Camera And Motion

Camera posture checks and desk setup scans are permission-gated. Camera imagery is intended for on-device posture and workspace feedback placeholders and is not uploaded by the current mock AI implementation.

Motion-related posture and sitting features are permission-gated placeholders. Motion signals are intended for local sitting and inactivity awareness only.

## Notifications

The app may schedule local reminders for posture checks, movement breaks, hydration prompts, stretch routines, and focus sessions when the user enables notifications.

## Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and account information under Apple's own terms and privacy policies. PosturePilot AI does not store payment card details.

## Remote AI

Mock AI is enabled by default. If a future production build enables a remote AI backend, requests should be routed through the developer's backend and this policy should be updated before release to explain what data is sent, why it is sent, and how it is protected. API keys must never be stored in the app.

## Wellness Disclaimer

PosturePilot AI is not a medical device. Posture and ergonomics insights are informational only and are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or injury prevention. Users should seek professional care for pain, injuries, or medical concerns.
