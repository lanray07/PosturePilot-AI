# Wellness Disclaimer

PosturePilot AI is a wellness and productivity app, not a medical device.

Posture scores, AI posture summaries, desk setup suggestions, sitting reminders, stretch prompts, and movement reminders are informational only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or injury prevention.

The app uses cautious language such as "possible slouch detected" and "consider adjusting screen height" because posture and ergonomics feedback can vary by person, environment, device placement, and context.

Seek professional care for pain, injuries, persistent discomfort, medical concerns, or questions about a health condition.
