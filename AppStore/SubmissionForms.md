```text
PosturePilot AI helps desk-heavy people build healthier posture and movement habits during work, study, gaming, and creative sessions.

Use mock AI posture checks, smart sitting awareness, focus timers, stretch routines, desk setup suggestions, and local insights to stay more aware of how you sit, move, and reset throughout the day.

Key features:
- Camera posture check placeholder with supportive posture cues
- Smart sitting timer and movement reminder scaffolding
- Focus sessions for work, study, gaming, and deep focus
- Stretch and recovery routines for desk fatigue
- Desk ergonomics scanner placeholder with setup suggestions
- Weekly posture trends, streaks, achievements, and insights
- StoreKit subscription scaffolding for Free, Pro, and Elite plans
- Apple Watch and WidgetKit placeholders for future companion experiences

PosturePilot AI is designed for remote workers, office workers, students, gamers, coders, creators, and anyone with a desk-heavy lifestyle.

Important: PosturePilot AI is not a medical device. Insights are informational only and are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or injury prevention. Seek professional care for pain, injuries, or medical concerns.
```
