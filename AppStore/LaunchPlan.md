# PosturePilot AI App Store Launch Pack

## Positioning

PosturePilot AI is a wellness and productivity app for people who live at a desk. The strongest angle is not perfection or medical correction; it is daily posture awareness, movement consistency, and a calmer desk routine.

Primary promise:

> Build healthier desk routines with posture checks, movement breaks, focus sessions, and ergonomic awareness.

Avoid:

- Medical diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention language
- Guaranteed pain reduction or injury prevention
- Incentivised ratings, review gating, or chart manipulation
- Keyword stuffing or competitor names in metadata

## Viral Loops

In-app loops added to support organic sharing:

- Dashboard share card for a friendly invite
- Insights share card for progress moments
- Achievement share card for milestone moments
- Settings share action for intentional sharing
- Native App Store review prompts after meaningful completions

Review prompts must remain neutral and use Apple's provided review API. Do not offer rewards for ratings or ask for a specific rating.
