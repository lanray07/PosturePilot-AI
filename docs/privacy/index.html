Privacy Policy

PosturePilot AI Privacy Policy

Effective May 29, 2026

Data Handling

The current version stores posture sessions, focus sessions, stretch routine completion, desk setup scan notes, achievements, preferences, and subscription state locally on the user's device with SwiftData.

Mock AI posture analysis is enabled by default. Camera posture checks and desk setup scans are permission-gated placeholders and are not uploaded by this build.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. PosturePilot AI does not store payment card details.

Remote AI

The submitted build does not send user data to a third-party AI provider. If a future production build enables remote AI, this policy will be updated before release.

Wellness Disclaimer

PosturePilot AI is not a medical device. Insights are informational only and are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or injury prevention.
